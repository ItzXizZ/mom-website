My Therapeutic Philosophy

Person-Centered Approach

I believe you are the expert in your own life. My role is to offer a safe, non-judgmental, and supportive space where you can explore your experiences and move toward healing at your own pace. Therapy is a collaborative process that honours your autonomy, strengths, and lived experience.

Biopsychosocial Framework

Healing is most effective when we consider the whole person. I work from a biopsychosocial perspective, recognizing how biological factors, emotional experiences, relationships, culture, and social context all interact to influence mental health and wellbeing.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)

Internal Family Systems (IFS) helps you build a compassionate relationship with all parts of yourself. This approach supports self-leadership, emotional regulation, and inner harmony, and is particularly effective in trauma recovery and personal growth.
