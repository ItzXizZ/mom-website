Welcome

Sorance Counselling provides professional counselling services grounded in ethical practice, evidence-informed approaches, and respect for the dignity and autonomy of each client.

Counselling is a collaborative process that invites reflection, understanding, and change within a safe and confidential therapeutic relationship. Services are offered with cultural humility, clinical integrity, and adherence to the ethical standards of the British Columbia Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC).

About Dr. Soraya Centeno, PhD, RCC-ACS, CCC

I am Dr. Soraya Centeno, PhD, a Registered Clinical Counsellor and Approved Clinical Supervisor (RCC-ACS), and a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) in good standing with the British Columbia Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC). My clinical practice is informed by advanced training in trauma-informed care, including formal certification in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) and specialized training in trauma-responsive therapeutic approaches.

My professional background includes over ten years of experience in mental health, trauma recovery, and family systems counselling across clinical, academic, and community-based settings. I have particular experience working with culturally diverse individuals, families, and newcomers.

Approach to Counselling

Client-Centred Practice

Counselling is guided by the client's goals, values, and lived experience. My role is to facilitate a respectful, non-judgmental, and professional therapeutic space that supports self-exploration, clarity, and informed decision-making.

Biopsychosocial Framework

Mental health is understood as shaped by biological, psychological, relational, cultural, and social factors. Counselling explores how these dimensions interact and influence wellbeing.

Internal Family Systems (IFS)-Informed Work

Where appropriate, I integrate principles of Internal Family Systems (IFS) to support self-awareness, emotional regulation, and internal coherence in a compassionate and non-pathologizing manner.

Trauma-Informed Practice

All services are delivered through a trauma-informed lens that prioritizes psychological safety, transparency, and client autonomy. EMDR may be used when clinically appropriate and mutually agreed upon.
