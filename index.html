Welcome

Welcome to Sorance Counselling, a place where you can feel supported, understood, and empowered on your journey toward healing and growth. Whether you're navigating life's challenges, recovering from difficult experiences, or simply seeking a space to better understand yourself, you don't have to face it alone.

Counselling is a collaborative process built on trust, compassion, and respect. Together, we'll create a safe and confidential space where you can explore your experiences, build on your strengths, and move toward meaningful, lasting change.

At Sorance Counselling, every person is welcomed with warmth, cultural humility, and genuine care. My practice is grounded in person-centred, trauma-informed, and evidence-informed approaches, while adhering to the ethical standards of both the British Columbia Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC) and the Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association (CCPA).

About Dr. Soraya Centeno, PhD, RCC-ACS, CCS

I am Dr. Soraya Centeno, PhD, a Registered Clinical Counsellor and Approved Clinical Supervisor (RCC-ACS) with the British Columbia Association of Clinical Counsellors (BCACC), and a Canadian Clinical Supervisor (CCS) with the Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association (CCPA).

My work supporting individuals, children, and families is grounded in a compassionate, person-centred, and trauma-informed approach. I have advanced training in trauma therapy, including certification in Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR), along with specialized training in trauma-responsive therapeutic approaches. I am committed to ongoing professional development and regularly engage in continuing education to ensure my practice reflects current research and best practices.

My experience spans clinical, academic, and community settings, supporting people through trauma, life transitions, and family challenges. I have extensive experience working with culturally diverse individuals, families, and newcomers, and I have had the privilege of supervising and mentoring counselling students and emerging clinicians as they develop their professional skills. Above all, I strive to create a safe, respectful, and collaborative space where clients feel heard, understood, and supported.

My Approach

I believe that every person has the capacity for growth and healing when they feel safe, supported, and truly heard. My role is to provide a compassionate, collaborative, and non-judgmental space where we can explore life's challenges together at a pace that feels right for you.

My work is grounded in a person-centred, trauma-informed approach, recognizing that our experiences are shaped by many factors, including our relationships, culture, life experiences, and overall wellbeing. Together, we'll explore what has brought you to counselling while building on your strengths and supporting meaningful, lasting change.

I also draw on Internal Family Systems (IFS)-informed principles to help clients better understand their thoughts, emotions, and inner experiences with curiosity and self-compassion. When appropriate, I integrate Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) as an evidence-based approach to processing trauma and reducing the impact of distressing experiences.

Above all, I believe counselling should feel like a partnership—one where you feel respected, empowered, and supported as you move toward the life you want to create.
